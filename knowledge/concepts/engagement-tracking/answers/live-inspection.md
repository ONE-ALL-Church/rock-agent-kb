# What live Rock records should I inspect for Engagement Tracking?

For Engagement Tracking, inspect these live surfaces before changing production behavior: Rock version, Security roles and permissions, WorkflowType and Workflow records, DataView and report filters, Group, GroupType, Location, and Schedule records, Communication recipient, medium, and send history.
