# What risks, caveats, or source-authority limits matter for Apple TV Apps?

A TVML text shadow can be specified with horizontal offset, vertical offset, blur radius, and color, but its surrounding element may clip the shadow vertically; keeping the shadow near the text reduces that risk. Rock's Lava API guidance identifies Apple TV and Roku channels as examples of custom APIs that can be built with Lava, but warns that Lava webhooks do not include security by default.
