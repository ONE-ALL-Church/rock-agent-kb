# What should I check first for Apple TV Apps?

Rock Apple TV is documented as a set-top extension of Rock RMS for TVML applications linked to Rock, and the Apple TV functionality requires Rock version 14 or greater. Apple TV pages in Rock must output valid TVML and can use Rock-provided Lava merge fields such as CurrentPerson, Context, Campuses, SiteStyles, and CurrentPage. Rock Apple TV documentation groups JavaScript command behavior as a core part of building TV applications, so TV app guidance should treat commands as part of navigation, media, utility, and demo workflows. A Rock Apple TV app is created as a Rock-managed TV application record under CMS configuration, with Rock-side settings that are distinct from the App Store name.
