# What risks, caveats, or source-authority limits matter for Platform Configuration?

Large Rock releases can carry accumulated change and post-release fixes, so upgrade planning should distinguish major-version validation from routine patch-release validation and verify current release notes before action. For platform configuration, treat community examples as non-authoritative until the live setting, plugin, and Rock version are verified.
