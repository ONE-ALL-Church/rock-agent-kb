# What should I check first for Check-In?

Start by separating eligibility, availability, label printing, and device behavior. Inspect the check-in configuration, group type, groups, locations, schedules, campus, person/family eligibility, and kiosk or mobile device settings before changing rooms. When a person or room is missing, prove whether the blocker is data view filtering, schedule windows, location capacity, security, or label/device configuration.
