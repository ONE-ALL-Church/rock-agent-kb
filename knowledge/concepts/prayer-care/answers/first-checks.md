# What should I check first for Prayer And Care?

Prayer requests configured for automatic approval become available to the prayer team immediately; otherwise an administrator must approve them before they appear in prayer sessions. Rock can require administrator approval before prayer requests enter team sessions, and it prioritizes urgent requests ahead of requests ordered from least prayed-for to most prayed-for. A prayer session records another prayer count when a team member views a request, and enabled team flagging can unapprove a request once the block's configured flag threshold is reached.
